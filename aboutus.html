About Us


Welcome to UniFlix! Choosing what to watch can take longer than the movie itself, so UniFlix lets your face do the choosing. Open the Mood Detector, look into your camera, and a machine learning model reads your expression (happy, sad, angry, surprised, fearful, disgusted or calm) right inside your browser.


Your mood is matched to genres that suit it, and UniFlix pulls live recommendations, ratings, cast and trailers from The Movie Database (TMDB). Nothing from your camera is uploaded or stored: all the face analysis happens on your own device.
